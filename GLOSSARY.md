# textkit

Small, dependency-free text utilities used by apps to prepare text for display and URLs.

## Language

**Slug**:
A lowercase, dash-separated form of a title that is safe to use in a URL.
_Avoid_: permalink, handle

**Excerpt**:
A shortened, single-line preview of a text that ends on a whole word and is marked as shortened.
_Avoid_: snippet, summary, truncation
