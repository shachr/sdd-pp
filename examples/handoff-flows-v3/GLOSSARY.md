# textkit

Tiny text utilities: small, dependency-free functions that turn text into other text or into facts about it.

## Language

**Reading time**:
The estimated number of whole minutes a reader needs to read a text at a given reading speed.
_Avoid_: read time, duration

**Word**:
A whitespace-separated run of characters that contains at least one letter or digit.
_Avoid_: token, term
